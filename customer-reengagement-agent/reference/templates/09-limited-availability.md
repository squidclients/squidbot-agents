## No-Response Follow-Up
Hi {{name}}, I'm going to release the remaining slots to the waitlist tomorrow since they usually go within a day. I wanted to give you first chance since you've been such a loyal client. If you'd like a spot, just text me back. If the timing doesn't work this month, I completely understand — we'll catch you next time.

## Why This Works
- Real scarcity (named provider, limited slots) creates genuine urgency
- "First access" before waitlist triggers reciprocity
- Complimentary consultation removes risk of trying new provider
