# Campaign 01: Past Customer Check-In
# Type: Relationship | Best for: Chiropractic, Wellness, Preventive Care

## SMS Opening
Hey {{name}}, it's Dr. {{lastName}} from {{business}}. It's been a minute — hope {{bodyPart}} is treating you well! Just thinking about you and wanted to check in. How have things been?

## Email Opening
Subject: How's the {{bodyPart}}, {{name}}?
Hi {{name}},
I was reviewing patient files this week and noticed it's been about {{timeSinceLastVisit}} since your last visit. That's actually a good sign — it likely means your {{bodyPart}} is in better shape than when you first came in!
I wanted to personally reach out and see how you're feeling. A lot of my patients find that as time passes, they start noticing little things — {{specificSymptom1}}, {{specificSymptom2}}, that kind of thing. Sometimes the body just needs a small tune-up to stay ahead.
No pressure at all. I just wanted to open the door. Reply here or text the office at {{phoneNumber}} if you'd like to chat.
Take care,
Dr. {{lastName}}
{{business}}

## Reply Follow-Up (Positive Response)
That's exactly what I expected, {{name}}. The {{specificSymptom}} is super common after your original issue — it's usually just the muscles guarding that old pattern. The good news is a quick {{service}} can usually reset it in one visit.
Would it be alright if I sent over a couple of options for a short check-in visit? Nothing major — just {{duration}} minutes to take a look and make sure everything is tracking properly.

## No-Response Follow-Up
Hey {{name}}, no worries at all if the timing isn't right. I'll leave the ball in your court — if that {{specificSymptom}} ever acts up or you just want a quick tune-up, you know where to find me. Hope you're doing well.

## Why This Works
- Opens with genuine care, not a sales pitch — personal reachout builds goodwill
- Names a specific, relatable symptom that validates experience
- Complimentary offer removes all financial friction
