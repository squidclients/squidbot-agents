# Campaign 04: Old Lead
# Type: Opportunity | Best for: Real Estate, Mortgage, High-Consideration Services

## SMS Opening
Hi {{name}}, {{yourName}} with {{business}}. We chatted back in {{month}} about possibly {{interest}}. Just wanted to touch base — are you still thinking about making a move, or did you decide to hold off?

## Email Opening
Subject: The market has shifted, {{name}}
Hi {{name}},
{{yourName}} from {{business}}. We connected a few months back when you were exploring the idea of {{interest}}. I remember you were weighing whether the timing was right.
I'm reaching out because the market has actually shifted in an interesting way since we last talked. {{marketData}}. That's a pretty good combination if you're on the fence.
I'm not here to push you — I just want to make sure you have current information. If you're still curious about what your options look like today, I can put together a fresh analysis. No strings attached.
{{yourName}}
{{business}}

## Reply Follow-Up (Positive Response)
Great question. {{relevantData}}. I can put together a detailed analysis that shows exactly where you'd land. Want me to run the numbers?

## Permission Message
I'll build out a full breakdown — it takes me about a day to put together properly. I'll include {{whatsIncluded}}. Completely free, whether you decide to move forward later or not. Should I go ahead and put it together for you?

## Offer Message
Here's what I'll deliver:
— {{deliverable1}}
— {{deliverable2}}
— {{deliverable3}}
— {{deliverable4}}
All complimentary, no obligation. If the numbers make sense, we can talk about next steps. If they don't, you'll still have the data.

## Booking Message
I'll need about {{duration}} minutes to walk you through the analysis once it's ready. I've got openings for a quick {{method}}:
{{day1}} at {{time1}} ({{method1}})
{{day2}} at {{time2}} ({{method2}})
{{day3}} at {{time3}} ({{method3}})
What works best? I'll have the full report ready by then.

## No-Response Follow-Up
Hi {{name}}, I know timing is everything with these things. If {{interest}} isn't on your radar right now, that's totally fine — I'll check back in a few months. If anything changes before then, you've got my number.

## Why This Works
- Leads with market intel, not a sales pitch
- Fresh analysis offer is genuinely useful regardless of intent
- Multiple meeting formats removes logistical friction
