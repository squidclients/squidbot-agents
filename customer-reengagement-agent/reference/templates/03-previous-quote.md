# Campaign 03: Previous Quote
# Type: Opportunity | Best for: Roofing, Solar, Renovation, Home Improvement

## SMS Opening
Hey {{name}}, {{yourName}} from {{business}} here. We came out {{lastVisitDate}} to look at your {{projectType}} — figured I'd check in and see if that project is still on your radar. How've you been?

## Email Opening
Subject: That {{projectType}} quote from {{lastVisitDate}}
Hey {{name}},
{{yourName}} from {{business}}. We came out to your place back in {{lastVisitMonth}} and put together a quote for the {{projectDetails}}. I know sometimes these projects get pushed down the list, so I wanted to check in and see where things stand.
A couple of things have changed since we last talked that might actually work in your favor. {{whatChanged}}.
If you're still thinking about the {{projectType}}, I'd love to share what's new. No pressure either way. Just reply here or call me at {{phoneNumber}}.
Thanks {{name}},
{{yourName}}
{{business}}

## Reply Follow-Up (Positive Response)
Glad to hear it's still on your mind, {{name}}. So here's what's changed — {{whatChanged}}. Long story short, I can put together an updated quote that gives you {{upgrade}} for the same price we quoted before. Mind if I send over the updated numbers?

## Permission Message
Before I put the numbers together, I want to make sure I'm giving you something useful. Are you still thinking about the full {{projectType}}, or would you want to explore a few options at different price points? I can build out a couple of scenarios if that helps.

## Offer Message
Here's what I've put together, {{name}}:
Option 1 — Updated Quote: {{option1Details}}. Price: ${{price1}}.
Option 2 — Value Package: {{option2Details}}. Price: ${{price2}}.
Both options include {{warranty}}. I'm holding these prices through the end of the month.

## Booking Message
If either of those works for you, the next step is simple — I'll come back out, do a final measurement to confirm everything, and we'll get you on the schedule. Installation would be about {{installTimeline}}. I've got site visit openings {{day1}} or {{day2}}. Either of those work for a quick {{duration}}-minute visit?

## No-Response Follow-Up
Hey {{name}}, I'll keep this brief. The upgraded materials at the original price point are available through the end of the month — after that, pricing goes back to standard. If the timing isn't right, no worries. If you want me to hold the quote, just say the word and I'll extend it for you.

## Why This Works
- Reframes stale quote as new opportunity (material upgrades at same price)
- Two pricing options gives customer agency
- Deadline creates urgency framed as helpful information
