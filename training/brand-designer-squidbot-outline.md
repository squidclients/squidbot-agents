# Training Outline: From No Agent to a Seasoned SquidBot Brand Designer

## Outcome

Install, season, calibrate, and safely use the full Brand Designer agent.

1. The problem: inconsistent creative work, design bottlenecks, and scattered brand knowledge.
2. MET filter: makes money through faster campaign output and stronger creative; creates efficiency through reusable context and QA; saves time by reducing repetitive briefing and revisions.
3. Before state: no agent, no canonical profile, no provider, and no approval workflow.
4. Install: customer repo command, environment setup, and `doctor` readiness check.
5. Seasoning: website, approved logos, brand documents, fonts, current work, inspiration, dislikes, audience, offers, and owner approval.
6. Calibration: one social post, one website hero, and one presentation cover; score and revise the profile.
7. Production workflow: brief, generate, inspect, revise, approval, and archive learning.
8. Use cases: social/ad creative, thumbnails, presentation visuals, website graphics, email headers, lead magnets, and resize/export packs.
9. Guardrails: secrets stay local, logos are never generated, evidence beats assumptions, and nothing auto-publishes.
10. First-win exercise: create one real asset tied to a current revenue, efficiency, or time-saving priority.

## Proposed resources after approval

Presentation deck, seasoning worksheet, intake checklist, three-brief calibration scorecard, prompt/brief template, and production QA checklist.
